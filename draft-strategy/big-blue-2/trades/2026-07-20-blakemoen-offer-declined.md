# Trade offer from BlakeMoen — 2026-07-20

**Status: Declined / countered, not accepted.**

**Offer received:** BlakeMoen sends 2026 picks 1.10 (via skywalsher) + 1.11 (via Mi-les) + 2.09
(via jrolles). We send our own 1.03.

**Analysis:** raw pick-value math favored accepting (three assets for one), but 1.03 sat inside
the clear top tier of the rookie class (Love/Tate/Tyson/Concepcion), while 1.10/1.11 landed in a
noticeably weaker tier. Declined in order to keep 1.03 for [KC Concepcion](../2026-rookie-draft.md)
rather than trade down for volume in a class with a sharp early cliff.

**Outcome:** kept 1.03, took Concepcion. BlakeMoen went on to independently acquire 1.10 (via
skywalsher) and 1.11 (via Mi-les) anyway and drafted Kenyon Sadiq + Denzel Boston with them — see
[roster 3](../teams/roster-03-blakemoen.md).
